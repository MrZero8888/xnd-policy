Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Xuong Noi Dung ("the app") is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data the app uses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the app uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, subtitles, playlists and publishing time, and reads basic channel and video status and statistics for the owner's own channels.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and server. The app does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect data from any other users.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
